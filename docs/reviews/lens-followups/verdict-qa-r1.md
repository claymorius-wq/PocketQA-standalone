## New findings
1. [P3] On New study, focus clears the dock frame but not the dock's own scrim, and the spec cannot see that scrim. `apps/mobile/app/builder/new-study.tsx:42,148,426,452,462,514`.
   - **Geometry.** While the "To save" hint shows (any incomplete form), the frame is about 128 px: hint 44, gap 4, dock 68, margin 12. The 200 px Scrim reaches about 72 px above the frame. It fades in from 72 px above the frame's top and is solid from 12 px above it. Without the hint it reaches 40 px up and is solid from 4 px.
   - **Effect.** The focus room ends 22 px above the frame (`DOCK_CLEARANCE`). An input that Tab finds partly below that edge is aligned to it. It then sits under a veil that rises from 0 to about 83 %, with its text under about 40 %. At the consent field's measured position (7dab6ef: its bottom is 76 px under the frame), its first line is under about 38-75 %. These figures are computed from the styles, not measured.
   - **Other screens.** Builder study (clearance 40, fade 32), Review (32, 32) and Sign in (14, 14; `apps/mobile/src/screens/authParts.tsx:103`) keep their clearance at least as tall as their scrim's fade. New study is the exception, and it is the screen the spec uses. SC 2.4.11 AA (not entirely hidden) still holds.
   - Fix: make New study's clearance cover its scrim's fade, or shorten the scrim. Measure the spec's bottom edge at the scrim's top, which needs a testID on the scrim.
2. [P3] 7dab6ef's text-area exception has a sound basis, but it checks less than its comment says and can fail on a correct build. `apps/mobile/e2e/focus-visible.spec.ts:61-65`.
   - **Basis (sound).** Chromium scrolls a focused `<input>`'s whole box into view, but only reveals a `<textarea>`'s caret. SC 2.4.11 AA asks only that focus is not entirely hidden. The consent result fits this rule.
   - **Weaker than stated.** The 20 px is counted from the box top, and 13 px of that is padding (`apps/mobile/src/ui/index.tsx:526-527`). So it proves only 6 px of the 22 px first line, not a full line. It is also measured to the frame (finding 1). It would therefore pass a box whose only clear strip lies under the solid scrim, which is an AA failure.
   - **Brittle at the top** (traced, not run). Shift+Tab can reach a text area while it sits above the view. Only its caret line is then aligned to the padding edge, so its box top lands about 13 px (dark) or 5 px (light) past the scrim bound, and line 65 fails on a correct build. It passes today only because objective and task-0 are in view when the upward walk reaches them.
   - Fix: on web, scroll a focused multiline Field's whole well into view with `scrollIntoView({ block: 'nearest' })`. That call honours scroll-padding, and the 138 px consent well fits. Then drop the exception. Short of that, check the first text line (box top + 13, 22 px tall) against the header scrim and the dock scrim's top.
3. [P3] The spec catches a missing scroll-padding because of where the controls fall, not by construction. `apps/mobile/e2e/focus-visible.spec.ts:26-43,56-71`.
   - **Without padding.** Chromium leaves a control that is fully in view where it is, even under the ~128 px dock band or the ~100 px header band. It moves a partly visible control only to the very edge. A walk through 13 controls will very probably leave one of them under the dock or header. The consent field, reached from below the view, lands with its first line at the bottom edge, under the dock. Nothing forces this, so the planned run with the padding off is the proof.
   - **Lookups.** The scrim and dock lookups are not scoped to the screen, and they fall back to 0 and ∞ when the element is missing (`:32-33,39-40`). A renamed testID would silently switch that side's check off. Every docked Screen now carries `screen-dock`, so a spec on Review pushed over Builder study would find the hidden frame beneath, or hit strict mode.
   - Fix: also assert the scroll container's computed `scroll-padding-top` and `scroll-padding-bottom` against the scrim's bottom and against the dock frame plus clearance. Fail when either element is missing, and scope both lookups to the screen.
4. [P3] Disputes' and Moderation's new binding and reopen have no test. `apps/mobile/app/(admin)/disputes.tsx:191,205-217`, `apps/mobile/app/(admin)/moderation.tsx:149,177-192`. Builder study, Review and Stuck are pinned. Fix: mirror the Stuck test for each screen.
5. [P3] Pre-existing: a reopened sheet starts as sent, but its fields stay editable while the request runs, so it can drift from what was sent. The new tests type into it on purpose. On success the sheet closes showing text the server never received; for admins that is the audited reason. `apps/mobile/app/(admin)/stuck.tsx:79`, `moderation.tsx:207`, `disputes.tsx:247-256`, `apps/mobile/app/builder/review/[id].tsx:212`. Fix: make the sheet's fields read-only while `isPending`.
6. [P3] Pre-existing, outside this diff: Credits' top-up reads the live amount when it runs, and the amount control stays enabled while the request is pending. `apps/mobile/src/screens/CreditsScreen.tsx:125,145-155,163`. On web, a top-up held offline sends the amount picked after the tap. This affects test credits only. Fix: use a variables-based mutationFn and call `topUp.mutate(Number(amount))`.
